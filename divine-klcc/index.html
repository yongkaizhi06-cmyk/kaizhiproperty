FAQ · 常见问答

Questions Buyers Ask
买家最关心的问题

Quick answers verified against the developer's official sales kit. For full details, request the 24-page Investor Guide.
资料来源：发展商官方销售手册。完整资料请下载 24 页投资指南。

How far is Divine KLCC from Petronas Twin Towers? · 离双子塔多远？ +
Divine KLCC is 400 metres from Petronas Twin Towers — approximately 1 minute walk through a covered walkway connecting to the Jalan Ampang–KLCC junction.
距离双子塔仅 400 米，约 1 分钟步行，经由通往 Jalan Ampang – KLCC 交汇处的有盖走道直达。
What is the starting price of Divine KLCC? · 起价多少？ +
Divine KLCC starts from RM 968,000 (SPA price) for Type A2 units of 502 sqft. Larger Type D penthouse units extend to approximately RM 3.83 million.
起价为 RM 968,000（SPA 价），适用于 502 sqft 的 A2 单位。较大的 D 型顶层单位价格约 RM 383 万。
Is Divine KLCC eligible for MM2H? · 是否符合第二家园资格？ +
Yes. Divine KLCC unit types cover all three MM2H tiers — Silver (RM 600K+), Gold (RM 1M+), and Platinum (RM 2M+). Eligibility also requires meeting non-property MM2H criteria including fixed deposit and minimum stay.
是的。Divine KLCC 单位价格涵盖 MM2H 全部三个等级 — 白银（RM 60 万+）、黄金（RM 100 万+）、白金（RM 200 万+）。申请人还须符合定存与最低居留天数等非房产条件。
When will Divine KLCC be completed? · 何时交房？ +
Divine KLCC is scheduled for vacant possession (VP) in Q2 2032. Buyers pay through progressive billing aligned with construction stages under Malaysia HDA Schedule H.
交房（VP）预计于 2032 年第二季度。买家依马来西亚 HDA Schedule H 规定，按工程进度分期付款。
Who is the developer of Divine KLCC? · 发展商是谁？ +
Divine KLCC is developed by Sinaran Urusjuta Sdn Bhd, a property arm of Chin Hin Group — a Bursa Malaysia-listed integrated builder conglomerate spanning building materials, construction engineering, property development, and home & living.
发展商为 Sinaran Urusjuta Sdn Bhd，隶属于马交所上市的 Chin Hin Group（成兴集团），业务涵盖建材、工程建设、房地产开发及家居生活。
Can foreigners buy Divine KLCC? What is the minimum price threshold? · 外国人能买吗？最低门槛？ +
Yes. Foreign buyers are eligible to purchase Divine KLCC units priced at RM 1,000,000 and above, in line with Malaysia's foreign ownership rules for Kuala Lumpur. Divine KLCC offers multiple unit configurations meeting this threshold across Type B, C, and D.
可以。外国买家可购买价值 RM 100 万以上的 Divine KLCC 单位，符合马来西亚吉隆坡外国人购房规定。B、C、D 型多款户型皆达到此门槛。
Does Divine KLCC offer rental management? · 是否提供租赁代管？ +
Yes. Owners may appoint a professional property management partner to handle tenancy and rental operations. Owners receive net rental income after management fees, with no day-to-day landlord responsibilities required.
有的。业主可委托专业物业管理合作伙伴处理租赁与出租事务。业主仅需收取扣除管理费后的净租金，无需亲力亲为。
How is parking allocated at Divine KLCC? · 车位怎么分配？ +
Parking allocation depends on unit type. KLCC-view units receive 1 to 3 carpark bays per unit. Non-KLCC view units do not come with carpark bays. Single carpark bays are located between Level 4 and Level 11.
车位依单位类型分配。面向 KLCC 视野的单位提供 1 至 3 个车位；非 KLCC 视野的单位不附带车位。单车位位于 4 至 11 楼。
How does payment work? Is it progressive billing? · 付款进度怎么算？ +
Yes. Divine KLCC buyers pay through progressive billing aligned with construction stages, regulated under Malaysia's Housing Development Act (HDA) Schedule H. Buyers do not pay the full price upfront — payment is released as each construction milestone is completed.
是的。Divine KLCC 采用马来西亚《房屋发展法令》(HDA) Schedule H 规定的工程进度付款方式。买家无需一次付清，每完成一个工程阶段才放款相应款项。
What about maintenance fees and service charges? · 维护费 + 服务费多少？ +
Maintenance fees and service charges are determined by the management corporation after vacant possession, based on actual operating costs of the facilities including security, common-area cleaning, lifts, infinity pool, and sky-bar. For up-to-date estimated figures, request the full sales kit from Kai Zhi Property.
维护费及服务费由管理机构于交房后依实际运营成本厘定，包含安保、公共区域清洁、电梯、无边际泳池、空中酒吧等。最新估算请向 Kai Zhi Property 索取完整销售资料。
