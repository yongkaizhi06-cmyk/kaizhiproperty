🚉 Ahead of the upgrade curve. Taman Wahyu sits on the KTM Batu Caves–Pulau Sebang line — whose Salak Selatan–Seremban stretch is being rebuilt under KVDT2, a ~RM4.48b government upgrade (new tracks, Siemens signalling, station works). As the line modernises, the whole line — Taman Wahyu included — is set to run faster & more frequently. Add a 1-stop MRT interchange, the future MRT3 corridor, and the developer's own Ekinaka station hub, 140m covered walkway & future flyover ramp — a true transit address, secured before the upgrades fully land.
