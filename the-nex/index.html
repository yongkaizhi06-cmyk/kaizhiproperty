Straight Answers

Buying industrial is a big move — let's get the facts right.

Who is this for — own-use or investment?
Both. It suits business owners who want their own freehold-of-operations space (warehouse, workshop, studio, office, cloud kitchen), and investors looking at industrial — a resilient, rental-friendly asset class in a prime PJ catchment. Tell me your goal and I'll show you the numbers honestly.
Leasehold & industrial title — what does that mean for me?
The NeX sits on leasehold land with industrial title — normal for stratified industrial hubs. It means the units are meant for business/industrial use, not residential living. I'll walk you through tenure, usage and resale realities with real examples — no sugar-coating.
When is completion, and what's the construction timeline?
Target completion is 2030 — roughly 48 months from your SPA date. It's a new launch (previewing now), so I'll keep you updated with the developer's official progress as it moves.
How much is it, and what's the entry price?
Pricing varies by type, floor and facing. The official price list (SOV) goes to registered buyers first — register or WhatsApp me and I'll send the latest indicative numbers for the unit you want.
Can a foreigner or company buy?
Yes. Companies can buy and may finance up to ~90%. Foreigners/PRs can purchase where the SPA price exceeds RM3mil, subject to state land-office consent. I'll confirm your exact eligibility and the paperwork step by step.
What are the running costs?
Estimated maintenance is about RM0.22 per sq ft (incl. sinking fund), charged on your allocated share units. I'll budget the all-in monthly figure with you — maintenance, financing and the lot — before you commit.
